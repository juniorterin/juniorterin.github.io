$Breath

NodeFirebaseGoogle CloudOAuthReactTailwindStyled ComponentsFramer Motion

Once again driven by my own frustration — after searching the app market for a guided breathing app and finding mostly paywalled subscriptions, limited features, and zero sense of community — I built my own. It comes with a full routine studio and what might honestly be the most complete interface out there right now. I added database support and Google login to make it easier to scale the project if it ever graduates from prototype to actual product. Motion libraries keep everything feeling smooth and fluid, with hardware-accelerated animations throughout. And I pulled it off on the tightest budget imaginable: Google Cloud Run for deployment, Cloudflare for DNS, Firebase for the database.
